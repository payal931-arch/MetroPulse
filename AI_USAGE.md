\# AI Usage



\## Overview



AI assistance was used during the development of the MetroPulse assessment project as a development and learning aid.



The project implementation, execution, testing, validation, analysis, and final review were performed as part of the development workflow.



\## Areas Where AI Assistance Was Used



AI assistance was used for:



\- Understanding the assessment requirements and breaking them into implementation steps.

\- Explaining Python, SQL, DuckDB, Git, and Streamlit concepts.

\- Troubleshooting development and execution issues.

\- Reviewing SQL transformations and data-quality checks.

\- Suggesting improvements to ingestion robustness, including retry handling and idempotent reruns.

\- Assisting with documentation structure and README preparation.

\- Reviewing dashboard organization and usability.

\- Helping interpret analytical outputs and identify areas requiring validation.



\## Human Validation



Generated suggestions were reviewed and tested during development.



The following were executed and validated locally:



\- Data ingestion scripts

\- DuckDB database rebuild

\- SQL transformations

\- Data-quality tests

\- Dashboard data export

\- Streamlit dashboard

\- Git repository workflow



Results shown in the project are based on the project's processed datasets and reproducible pipeline rather than fabricated or manually entered analytical results.



\## Transparency



AI assistance was used as a supporting development tool. It was not used to represent unverified results as measured facts.



Where AI-generated suggestions were used, they were reviewed and adapted to the project's actual data, implementation, and assessment requirements.
